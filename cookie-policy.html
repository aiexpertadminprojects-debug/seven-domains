Cookie Policy

Draft explanation of cookies and similar technologies on Seven Domains.

Effective date: 10 November 2026. Update this page after the website analytics, advertising and consent tools are selected.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored by a browser. Similar technologies may store preferences, measure site usage or support security.

2. Cookie categories

  • Strictly necessary: required for core site functions, security or session management.
  • Preferences: remember language or display settings where implemented.
  • Analytics: help understand aggregate site usage if enabled.
  • Advertising: may be used by advertising partners if enabled and legally permitted.

3. Consent and controls

Non-essential cookies should not be activated until the required consent is obtained. Users can also manage cookies through browser settings. A consent banner and preference centre must be integrated before analytics or advertising cookies are deployed where required.

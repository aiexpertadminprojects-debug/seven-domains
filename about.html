About Seven Domains

Seven Domains is being built to make domain and hosting essentials easier to understand and manage.

Our purpose

Seven Domains aims to provide a clear starting point for individuals, creators and businesses building an online presence. The planned service catalogue includes domain registration through authorised provider partnerships, web hosting, DNS management and practical security options.

Our current stage

The website is currently a frontend launch preview. Domain registration, hosting provisioning, customer accounts, billing and DNS changes are not yet connected to production providers. We will publish confirmed service terms and prices before accepting orders.

Our principles

  • Transparent first-term and renewal pricing.
  • Clear separation of domain and hosting charges.
  • Security claims based on implemented and tested controls.
  • Accessible support and clear customer communication.

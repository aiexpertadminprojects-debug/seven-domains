GOOD TO KNOW

Questions,
made simple.

Can I register a domain right now?+

Not yet. This first build is a design preview. Live domain availability and registration require an authorised registrar/provider API.

Are hosting plans available for purchase?+

The prices are proposed launch prices. Hosting activation and payment will be enabled only after a hosting provider and service limits are confirmed.

Are domain charges included in hosting?+

No. Domain registration, renewal and transfer will be priced separately by extension.

Will Seven Domains offer .in and global domains?+

That is the planned catalogue. Actual availability depends on registrar accreditation, registry agreements and provider coverage.
